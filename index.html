About

A different kind of
HRIS & People Analytics specialist.

"I've sat on both sides of the HR system — the one asking for the report, and the one building it."

I sit at the point where HR, IT, and leadership stop understanding each other — and translate. At Expleo Germany I was the bridge between a legacy SAP HCM estate and a new global Workday tenant: mapping data structures, reconciling headcounts across international hubs, and building the CHRO-level Power BI suite that made workforce trends visible in real time.

Before that, at Infoplus, I built the company's first automated diversity dashboard and the data-quality routines behind it — the kind of unglamorous governance work that makes every report after it trustworthy.

This week, I'm completing Ironhack's AI Data Analytics Bootcamp in Berlin — adding Python, SQL, Tableau, and applied ML to that toolkit, alongside continued hands-on work in Workday sandbox tenants.
