About

A different kind of
HRIS specialist.

"I know what a broken tenant looks like at 6pm before a go-live — because I've had to fix one."

My career started on the SAP side of HR — the legacy systems most Workday consultants only ever hear about secondhand. At Expleo Germany, that history made me the natural bridge during a global SAP-to-Workday migration: I mapped legacy data structures, reconciled headcounts across international hubs, and stayed on after go-live to own Workday Core HCM and Recruitment across the EMEA region.

Before that, at Infoplus, I built the company's first automated diversity dashboard and the data-quality routines behind it — the unglamorous governance work that makes every report after it trustworthy.

I'm currently deepening my analytics toolkit — Python, SQL, Tableau, applied ML — through Ironhack's AI Data Analytics Bootcamp in Berlin, while still working hands-on in Workday sandbox tenants.
